\textbf{Pool.} At the last step 86.3\% (CMNIST) and 79.3\% (C-CIFAR10) of the saved pool images are bias-conflicting,
but only 31.2\% on BFFHQ, where the pool is mostly bias-aligned faces that $f_b$ finds hard.
\textbf{Generator.} Its outputs keep the class for $f_d$ (91.8--100\%) and move $f_b$ to the target class $k$
(99.8\% CMNIST, 72.5\% C-CIFAR10, 73.4\% BFFHQ). The probe agrees less often: it finds the bias of $k$ on 44.1\% and
33.2\% of the CMNIST and C-CIFAR10 outputs and the original bias on 13.1\% and 18.4\%, so the generator removes the
original bias more often than it installs the target one. On BFFHQ the edit is smaller (mean $|\Delta|$ 30.0 vs.\

keeps the class on all of them; on CMNIST the probe still finds the original color on 99.4\%: PGD attacks $f_b$,
not the bias attribute. \textbf{Mixup} outputs keep the class for $f_d$ (94.7--99.8\%) while $f_b$ predicts the
class on only 15.0\%, 24.4\% and 58.6\%.
