\textbf{Clear contributions.} Removing LfF reweighting costs the most ($-8.4$ on average; $-11.1$ on CMNIST and
$-9.4$ on BFFHQ). Removing the generator costs $-7.1$, almost all on CMNIST ($-20.2$); on BFFHQ the run without the
generator is slightly higher (71.0 vs.\ 70.0). Replacing the learned generator by random Gaussian noise of the same
visible size costs $-4.4$ ($-6.4$ on C-CIFAR10), and generating from batch images instead of pool images costs
$-4.1$ on CMNIST and $-2.5$ on C-CIFAR10.

\textbf{Weak or mixed evidence.} Mixing pool images instead of repeating them helps on C-CIFAR10 ($+3.6$) and
BFFHQ ($+2.2$) but not on CMNIST ($+0.1$). A random color edit plus noise is only $1.3$ points below the learned
generator on average ($-0.5$ on CMNIST, $-2.4$ on C-CIFAR10, $-1.0$ on BFFHQ), so most of the generator's gain is
explained by color-like edits on pool images. The color-only generator (without learned filters and noise) is worse
on CMNIST ($-14.7$) but better on C-CIFAR10 ($+5.2$) and BFFHQ ($+7.2$); its average is within one point of the
full model. The untargeted generator is below the targeted one on CMNIST ($-3.5$) and about equal on C-CIFAR10
($-0.5$). Without pool PGD, CMNIST drops by $1.5$.
