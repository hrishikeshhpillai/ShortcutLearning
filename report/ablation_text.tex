\textbf{Large effects.} Every variant lowers the average. Removing LfF reweighting costs the most ($-11.3$; $-14.4$
on CMNIST, $-13.6$ on BFFHQ). Removing the generator costs $-7.9$, mostly on CMNIST ($-20.3$; $-1.9$ on C-CIFAR10,
$-1.4$ on BFFHQ). Keeping only the generator's color matrix and residual (no learned filters and noise) costs $-4.7$,
again mostly on CMNIST ($-14.9$), while C-CIFAR10 is slightly higher ($+1.4$). Repeating pool images instead of
mixing them costs $-3.0$, most on C-CIFAR10 ($-5.3$).

\textbf{Small effects.} An untargeted generator ($-1.5$), pool PGD removed ($-0.9$), the generator on batch images
($-0.6$) and PGD on batch images ($-0.2$) change the average by less than two points. Their effects differ by
benchmark: drawing the generator's or PGD's images from the pool instead of the batch helps on CMNIST ($+5.2$ and
$+3.5$) but is worse on C-CIFAR10 ($-2.9$ and $-2.1$); pool PGD helps on C-CIFAR10 ($+3.0$) and changes CMNIST and
BFFHQ by $0.2$. All ablations are single runs (seed 0), and Table~\ref{tab:seeds} shows seed-to-seed spreads of
0.8--1.1 points for PoolForge, so the differences in this paragraph are within run-to-run variation.
