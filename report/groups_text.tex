\textbf{CMNIST.} On the 8{,}994 conflicting test images PoolForge is right and LfF wrong on 1{,}449, the reverse on
466. The best groups (digit 1 with colors 2 and 3, digit 0 with color 8) are at 97--100\%; the worst are digit 3 with
color 6 and digit 5 with colors 3 and 4 (44--61\%), still above LfF on the same groups (38--48\%).
\textbf{C-CIFAR10.} The two methods are close: 1{,}609 images favor PoolForge, 1{,}533 favor LfF and 3{,}867 are
wrong for both. The best groups are car with brightness, truck with pixelate and ship with saturate (70--76\%; LfF
69, 78 and 49); the worst are deer with fog (7\% vs.\ 24\% for LfF), bird with spatter (8 vs.\ 4) and deer with
elastic (12 vs.\ 7).
\textbf{BFFHQ} has two conflicting groups. PoolForge is better on young men (72.4 vs.\ 55.2) and about equal on old
women (72.0 vs.\ 73.2); 99 images favor PoolForge and 59 favor LfF.
