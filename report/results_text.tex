\textbf{Main benchmarks.} PoolForge has the highest average test bias-conflicting accuracy (\PFavg\%, against
\LFFavg\% for LfF, Table~\ref{tab:main}). It is the best method on CMNIST (84.4 vs.\ 75.6 for LDD and 73.3 for LfF)
and on BFFHQ (72.2 vs.\ 64.2 for LfF and LC). On C-CIFAR10 it is second (40.0), behind LC (41.4) and ahead of LfF
(39.2). \textbf{Three seeds} (Table~\ref{tab:seeds}): over seeds 0--2 PoolForge averages $65.0\pm0.8$ against
$56.2\pm2.1$ for LfF ($+8.8$), with a smaller spread than LfF on every benchmark.
\textbf{Worst group} (Table~\ref{tab:other}): PoolForge raises the worst group from 4.2 (LfF) to 44.1 on CMNIST and
from 4.0 to 7.0 on C-CIFAR10; on BFFHQ it is below LfF (52.4 vs.\ 55.2).
\textbf{Unbiased accuracy} on BFFHQ is lower than for every baseline (63.9 vs.\ 75.6 for LfF and 75.8 for Vanilla):
the gain on bias-conflicting faces is paid for with bias-aligned faces.
\textbf{Bias ratios} (Table~\ref{tab:ratio}): PoolForge is above LfF at five of six ratios, with the largest gaps
at the strongest bias (0.5\%: $+23.6$ on CMNIST, $+11.0$ on C-CIFAR10); at C-CIFAR10 5\% it is below LfF
(53.1 vs.\ 58.9).
